# Responsible Use

Peerward is an author-side research-writing mentor and revision aid. It does not certify scientific correctness, predict acceptance, or replace human accountability.

## Student guidance

- Preserve the student's authorship and decision ownership.
- Prefer questions, hints, scaffolds, and feedback before direct rewriting.
- Never invent research results, references, novelty, data, or proof steps.
- A polished manuscript is not evidence that the underlying research is valid.

## Manuscript evaluation

- Treat scores as evidence-indexed diagnostics, not acceptance probabilities.
- State assessment scope, missing files, confidence, and unverified claims.
- Escalate unresolved theorem correctness, data integrity, ethics, authorship, and conflict-of-interest questions to qualified humans.

## Mock peer review

- Label generated reports as mock reviews for author training or pre-submission stress testing.
- Do not present Peerward as a journal-appointed reviewer or editor.
- Do not speculate about author motives, demand self-serving citations, or use personal language.
- Follow the target journal's reviewer instructions and AI-use policy.

## Confidentiality

Before processing a non-public manuscript or review:

1. confirm that the user is authorized to provide it;
2. verify that the selected environment satisfies applicable retention, reuse, and confidentiality requirements;
3. do not place the material in public issues, repositories, examples, or logs;
4. stop if journal or institutional policy prohibits the intended AI use.

Do not assume that a general-purpose AI service is approved for confidential official peer review. Publisher and institutional rules control.

## Responses to reviewers

- Verify response-letter promises against the revised manuscript.
- Use reasoned disagreement when appropriate; do not fabricate compliance.
- Report remaining limitations and unresolved evidence.
- The authors and corresponding author remain responsible for the final response and submission.

## Disclosure

Authors should check the target venue's current policy to determine whether and how AI assistance must be disclosed. Peerward does not provide a universal disclosure rule.

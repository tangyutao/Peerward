# Response-Letter Quality Rubric

Use this reference to draft, evaluate, or verify a response to reviewers. Evaluate the response letter and revised manuscript together whenever possible.

## Verification statuses

For every comment assign one:

- verified: the promised manuscript change is present and addresses the concern;
- partial: a relevant change exists but does not fully resolve the concern;
- response-only: the response promises or explains an action not verified in the manuscript;
- not-found: the claimed change or location cannot be found;
- uncertain: available evidence cannot determine coverage.

## Critical failures

Any of these prevents a high-quality rating:

- a reviewer comment is omitted or silently merged;
- the response claims a theory, experiment, comparison, or correction that is absent;
- a factual disagreement is asserted without evidence;
- the response changes tone but not the manuscript defect;
- the rebuttal misquotes or broadens the reviewer;
- locations, equation numbers, figures, or section references are wrong;
- confidential or personal attacks appear.

## 100-point rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Coverage and traceability | 15 | Is every comment represented and mapped to an action or reasoned disagreement? |
| Concern understanding | 15 | Does the response address the underlying scientific risk rather than keywords alone? |
| Scientific adequacy | 20 | Is the chosen theory, experiment, comparison, explanation, or scope correction sufficient? |
| Manuscript verification | 20 | Are promised changes actually present and consistent in the revised manuscript? |
| Evidence and location | 10 | Are claims, locations, new results, and supporting evidence precise? |
| Professional tone | 10 | Is the response respectful, direct, and non-defensive? |
| Boundary and proportionality | 5 | Does it avoid overclaiming what the revision establishes? |
| Cross-comment consistency | 5 | Do responses, manuscript changes, symbols, and claims agree across the letter? |

Score each dimension 0–4 using the anchors in [manuscript-quality-rubric.md](manuscript-quality-rubric.md). Calculate each contribution as `(score / 4) × weight`, and pair every score with evidence.

## Quality bands

- **90–100, Verified and persuasive:** all material promises verified; no critical failure.
- **75–89, Strong but incomplete:** generally sound, with bounded coverage or evidence gaps.
- **60–74, High revision risk:** several responses are partial, response-only, or weakly reasoned.
- **Below 60, Not ready:** missing coverage, unverified promises, or unprofessional reasoning.

A polished tone cannot compensate for an unfulfilled manuscript change.

## Per-comment response structure

1. **Acknowledge:** identify the legitimate issue without exaggerated agreement.
2. **Interpret:** state the concern being addressed.
3. **Act:** describe the concrete manuscript change or reasoned disagreement.
4. **Locate:** give section, equation, figure, table, or line information.
5. **Validate:** explain what evidence now resolves the concern.
6. **Bound:** state remaining limitation when the action is narrower than the request.

For disagreement:

- separate factual error, scope choice, and alternative scientific judgment;
- provide evidence and reasoning;
- improve the manuscript where the misunderstanding reveals an exposition problem;
- remain courteous and allow the editor to evaluate the disagreement.

## Required outputs

Return:

1. coverage matrix by reviewer and comment;
2. verification status for every promise;
3. dimension scores with evidence;
4. critical failures and overclaims;
5. corrected response suggestions;
6. manuscript changes still required;
7. final readiness judgment and its limits.
